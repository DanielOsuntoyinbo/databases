--- Analysis ---

Remote floor (~23ms w:1, ~45ms w:majority) is roughly 2x local floor
(~3ms w:1, ~20ms w:majority) — consistent with paying the cross-region
network hop twice (client -> remote mongos -> shard primary -> back)
instead of once. Remote latency stays flat across concurrency 10-50;
local latency climbs with load — the fixed double-hop network cost
dominates at these concurrency levels and swamps any local queueing
effect. w:majority adds a consistent ~17-20ms offset on top of both
local and remote floors (shard1's own replication-wait cost),
confirming that cost is shard-internal and independent of which mongos
was used.
