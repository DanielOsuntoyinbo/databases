--- Analysis ---

This is the concrete demonstration of why MongoDB recommends odd
vote counts. With 4 votes split 2-and-2 across two regions, a network
partition between those regions leaves BOTH sides at exactly 2 votes —
neither reaches the 3 needed for majority. The cluster doesn't fail
ungracefully; it correctly and repeatedly refuses to elect a primary
it can't legitimately claim majority support for. Every node stays
healthy throughout — this is availability lost to correct split-brain
protection, not to any failure.
