--- Analysis ---

Complete regional outage — router, config server, and data shard all
simultaneously unreachable — produced zero application-visible
impact for a properly configured (seed-list) client. Every layer of
the sharded cluster (CSRS, shard, mongos routing) independently held
majority/redundancy because each was distributed across all 3 regions
rather than concentrated in any one. This is the capstone result for
the sharded cluster section of the talk.
